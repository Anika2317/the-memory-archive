    About the Archive

    Alzheimer's changes what someone remembers, not who they are.

    The Memory Archive is a San Diego community project collecting stories about people living with Alzheimer's and related dementias, told by the families and friends who know them best, or in their own words.

    When someone is diagnosed with Alzheimer's, conversations often narrow to symptoms and care. Yet a life is made of specific things: a song on the car radio, a recipe written on an index card, a Sunday walk in North Park, a joke told the same way for forty years. Like the five petals of a forget-me-not, those details belong together.

    In this archive:

    • Memories link to one another through shared songs, places, decades, and details
    • Each month, families across San Diego answer a single shared question
    • Every memory is shared with permission, and families can remove theirs at any time
